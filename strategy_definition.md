StrategyName: EMA Trend Continuation
––––––––––
Idea behind the trading strategy: 
Price is trending upward.
Price starts to show a consolidation pattern.
Volume contracts during the pullback.
Volume expands above average volume during continuation.
Enter when breaking out of the consolidation pattern.
––––––––––
Trading type: Options
Position holding: Maximum position holding time = ~14-21 days; vast majority of cases will hold for less
––––––––––
Timeframes involved in entry: 1 Week, 1 Day, 5 Minute
Timeframe confluences- 
	Weekly: Price above 21EMA > 50EMA; but even stronger sign if above 8EMA as well
	Daily: Price above 21EMA > 50EMA
	5 Minute: Pullback into 8EMA
Uptrend = 8EMA> 21EMA> 50EMA
Average volume = 20 MA volume
––––––––––
Entry Conditions-
1. Weekly trend bullish; Price above 21EMA > 50EMA; but even stronger sign if above 8EMA as well
2. Daily trend bullish; Price above 21EMA > 50EMA
3. 5m Price above 21 EMA
4. Volume decreases during pullback in daily timeframe
5. Pullback to close to 8 EMA or strong break of resistance trendline (made in daily TF) with high volume (both in 5 min TF)
6. Volume above 20 MA volume
7. Bullish candle forms in daily
––––––––––
Volume Candle Rules-
Volume should decrease during 1D consolidation
Volume on breakout should be > Average volume
––––––––––
Profit targets: 2R on trades with medium-high confidence, 1.5R on trades with lower confidence. Risk amount should be the distance to 21 ema from entry point.
Exit trade: when 1D candle is below 8EMA 15 minutes before market close
––––––––––
Avoid stocks w/ price < $5.00
Volume avg should be > 500,000
––––––––––

1. When looking at the daily chart, entry MUST be on atleast the 7th candle of consolidation, meaning atleast 6 candles of consolidation before the entry on the 7th candle. More days than that is acceptable though
